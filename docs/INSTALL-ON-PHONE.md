# Put Ride Day on your phone

No app store. You add the app from the browser and it opens full screen from your home screen like any other app. You need the **app link** and the **team code** from a trip leader.

## iPhone

1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**. (Not in the list? Tap **Edit Actions** at the bottom and add it.)
4. Tap **Add**.
5. Open **Ride Day** from your home screen and sign in: team code → your name → choose a 4-digit PIN.

The home-screen app has its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so sign in from the home-screen icon.

## Android

1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install** if a banner appears. Otherwise tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) → **Install**.
3. Open **Ride Day** and sign in: team code → your name → choose a 4-digit PIN.

## Allow when asked

- **Camera and photos**: to add photos, receipts and release forms.
- **Microphone**: to record portrait interviews.
- **Location**: only attached when you check in or report something, if you leave the box ticked.

## Hand in your forms

Go to **More → My forms**. For each form (waiver, media release, insurance card…) tap **Upload** and take a photo of each page or pick a PDF. If a leader asks for a new copy you'll get a message, and the form shows what's needed. Only you and the trip leaders can see your forms.

## Morning reminders

Every morning the app sends your jobs for the day: driving the van, stayovers to call, the blog post and so on.

- **Email**: make sure a leader has your email in the Riders list.
- **Phone notification** (optional): install the free **ntfy** app (search "ntfy" in the App Store or Google Play, or see [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh)), tap **+**, and subscribe to the topic name your leader puts next to your name in the Riders list. Anyone who knows the topic can read it, so keep it hard to guess.

Inside the app, the **Today** tab always shows "Your jobs today", and the icon shows a badge with how many are open.

## Tips for the road

- **No signal is fine.** Everything you add is saved on the phone and uploads by itself when you're back online. *More → Settings & sync* shows what's waiting.
- **Videos** upload in pieces; if you close the app, the upload continues next time you open it. On a weak connection you can pause uploads there and resume on Wi-Fi.
- **Photo size**: *Standard* (default) makes photos about 1 MB so they upload fast. Choose *Original* in Settings to keep full size.
- **Long interviews**: the app saves the recording as it goes, but your phone's own recorder (Voice Memos) is the safest for anything over 20 minutes. Upload it afterwards with *Audio file*.
- **Forgot your PIN?** Ask a leader to reset it.
